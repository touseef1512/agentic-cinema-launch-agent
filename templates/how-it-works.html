How it Works

Launch Radar runs a 3-step autonomous pipeline to build your release strategy. Every recommendation is traceable to real, currently-active web data—not invented by an AI.

01 Search & Discovery

We query the web for active festivals, distribution companies, and sales agents currently accepting submissions for your specific genre and budget tier.

02 Find All & Extract

We crawl the top results to extract real, unstructured submission criteria, deadlines, and contact information directly off each candidate's website.

03 Gemini Synthesis

A specialized Gemini agent acting as a distribution strategist cross-references your film's profile against the extracted web data. It returns a fully ranked strategy memo detailing exact fit reasons and the actions required.
